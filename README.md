# New York Life (new-york-life)

New York Life Insurance Company is the largest mutual life insurance company in the United States, headquartered in Manhattan, New York. The company provides life insurance, fixed and variable annuities, long-term care, and investment management (through subsidiaries such as New York Life Investments / NYLIM). New York Life has maintained the highest financial-strength ratings from major rating agencies for more than a century.
